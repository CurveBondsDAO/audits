# Suggested Changes to YieldNest Protocol’s Economic Policies

---

## 🎯 Objective

The purpose of these proposed changes is to:

- Strengthen YND’s value accrual framework  
- Enhance transparency and institutional confidence  
- Establish clearly defined capital allocation policies  
- Position YieldNest for large-scale institutional capital participation  

---

## 1. 📊 Transparency & Reporting Standards

### Recommendation

Implement a formalized transparency and reporting framework covering:

- All protocol revenue sources  
- All Treasury-controlled accounts and wallets  

### Reporting Requirements

Periodic reporting (real-time or scheduled) of:

- Revenue inflows  
- Treasury balances  
- Capital deployment  

### Disclosure Requirements

Clear breakdown of:

- Revenue utilization  
- Retained vs deployed capital  

### Rationale

Institutional investors require:

- Verifiable and auditable revenue streams  
- Clearly defined treasury ownership structures  
- Transparent value accrual mechanisms  

### Expected Outcome

- Improved investor confidence and credibility  
- Reduced information asymmetry and perceived risk  
- Alignment with institutional due diligence standards  

---

## 2. 🏦 Treasury Structure Formalization

### Recommendation

Formally define and disclose:

- Official Treasury wallets and accounts  

### Functional Role Clarity

Clearly define the roles of:

- Custody wallets  
- Execution wallets  
- Fee-receiving wallets  

### Rationale

A clearly articulated treasury structure is essential for:

- Capital allocation clarity  
- Governance accountability  
- Institutional capital onboarding  

---

## 3. 🌊 Protocol-Owned Liquidity (PoL) Strategy

### Recommendation

Allocate **30%–50% of idle treasury capital** into Protocol-Owned Liquidity (PoL):

- **60% → YND / ynETHx**  
- **40% → YND / STAK or YND / ynRWAx**  

---

### Pool Design & Fee Configuration

#### YND / ynETHx Pool

- **Fee Tier:** 0.5%  

**Role:**

- Core liquidity layer  
- Primary trading pair  
- High-volume execution environment  

---

#### YND / STAK or YND / ynRWAx Pool

- **Fee Tier:** 2.0%  

**Role:**

- High-fee capture layer  
- Arbitrage and rebalancing monetization  
- IL compensation mechanism  

---

## 4. 📐 Concentrated Liquidity Deployment (Uniswap V3/V4)

### Design Principle

All liquidity should be deployed as:

> Wide-range, YND-denominated concentrated liquidity positions optimized for maximum YND exposure

---

### 4.1 YND / ynETHx Pool (0.75% Swap Fee)

#### Price Definition

- YND per ETH  

#### Deployment Range

- **Lower Bound:** ~26.3M YND per ETH *(−75% downside)*  
- **Current Price:** ~6.5M YND per ETH  
- **Upper Bound:** ~435K → 261K YND per ETH *(15x–25x upside)*  

---

### 4.2 YND / STAK or ynRWAx Pool (2.0% Swap Fee)

#### Price Definition

- YND per STAK / ynRWAx  

#### Deployment Range

- **Lower Bound:** ~3,636 YND *(−50% downside)*  
- **Current Price:** ~1,818 YND  
- **Upper Bound:** ~91 → 61 YND *(20x–30x upside)*  

---

### 🧠 Strategic Implication

This configuration:

- Keeps positions active across extreme market conditions  
- Maximizes time-in-range  
- Minimizes need for active rebalancing  

---

### 💡 Capital Efficiency Advantage

This design enables the protocol to deploy substantially more YND than non-native treasury assets.

**Resulting in:**

- YND-dominant liquidity  
- Reduced reliance on ETH or stable assets  
- Higher capital efficiency per unit of treasury  

---

## 5. ⚖️ Dual-Pool Arbitrage Framework

### Structure

- **Volatile Pool:** YND / ynETHx  
- **Quasi-Stable Pool:** YND / STAK or YND / ynRWAx  

### Mechanism

- ETH price movement creates continuous arbitrage opportunities  
- Arbitrageurs rebalance pools  
- Protocol captures fees on every trade  

### Expected Outcome

Under sufficiently high swap fee conditions:

> Protocol-Owned Liquidity (PoL) revenue is expected to exceed impermanent loss (IL)

---

## 6. 🔁 Revenue Allocation Policy

### Recommendation

Standardize protocol revenue allocation:

- **30% – 50% → Protocol-Owned Liquidity (PoL)**  
- **≥ 20% → YND Buy & Burn**  

### Rationale

This framework:

- Strengthens liquidity depth  
- Creates direct token value accrual  
- Aligns protocol growth with tokenholder outcomes  

---

## 7. 🔥 Buyback & Burn Framework

### Recommendation

Implement a transparent, on-chain buyback and burn system:

- Designated execution wallets  
- Publicly verifiable transactions  
- Defined cadence or automated triggers  

### Rationale

A visible buyback system:

- Aligns with stated tokenomics  
- Enhances market credibility  
- Improves institutional investability  

---

## 8. 📘 Documentation Alignment

### Recommendation

If buybacks and revenue distribution are not expected in the near term:

Update documentation to reflect:

- Current economic model  
- Revised strategy  
- Implementation timelines  

### Rationale

Clear documentation is critical for:

- Institutional due diligence  
- Governance transparency  
- Market integrity  

---

If implemented, these changes would:

- Establish YND as a transparent, yield-generating digital asset  
- Enable institutional-grade capital deployment  
- Create a self-reinforcing liquidity and revenue flywheel  
- Align protocol operations with stated tokenomics
